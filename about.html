Our Story

Intelitek was founded with a single mission: to bring innovative automation and engineering solutions to industries worldwide. Over two decades, we have grown from a small engineering consultancy into a trusted partner for manufacturers, process industries, and infrastructure projects.

Our team of expert engineers, designers, and technicians combines deep technical knowledge with practical experience. We understand that every client has unique challenges, and we tailor our solutions to deliver measurable results.
